01

Hearth

Suggested starting point

A warm, familiar planner. Today’s agenda anchors the screen, with a peek at everyone’s week, school timetables and a few useful home controls alongside.

Best for everyday family life. A balanced overview; the full weekly calendar is one tap away.

02

Weekline

The whole family’s week on one screen. Follow a person’s row, spot a busy afternoon, and add an activity without losing sight of everyone else.

Best for coordinating busy schedules. More information at once; strongest on a larger landscape tablet.

03

Together

Everyone gets their own little space. A personal color, the next activity, and a small way to help at home. Friendly enough for the whole family to join in.

Best for involving the kids. Personal ownership is clear; comparing the whole week takes another tap.

04

Afterglow

A calmer companion for the family room. A big clock, one clear next step, a few reminders and the state of the house. Read it from across the room.

Best for an always-on display. Keeps the overview quiet; detailed planning happens in the calendar.

01 / GLANCE

Know what’s next.

Start on the overview. Open a school timetable, check the car, or see who’s doing pickup. Each concept gives those answers a different emphasis.

02 / TAKE PART

Make a little plan.

Filter the calendar by person. Add an event, assign a task or add to the shopping list. Tick something off and see it update across the page.

03 / FEEL AT HOME

Control the whole home.

Explore rooms, scenes, heating, blinds, locks, alarms, water, plugs, charging, energy, routines and system health. Try the offline state, too.

All readings, people, schedules and school images are examples. Changes last until reload. Security controls use demo PIN 1234. These concepts do not connect to your home or send anything.
