# School timetables

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing School board and dashboard previews.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each person can use saved timetable pages or a selected day's calendar agenda on School, defaulting to today, with the full calendar week available on the dashboard and in its expanded view.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Afterglow's system typography, semantic palette and existing form controls. Each calendar card on School uses one outer glass shell around its heading, top weekday tabs and selected-day agenda. The shell, full-week timetable frames and day popups share the stronger illuminated glass rim, blur and outer depth when backdrop filtering is available. Muted tab surfaces distinguish selection, while a Today text marker identifies the actual current date independently. Positive meeting counts use a primary-colored badge. Apply this local exception on School, dashboard previews and the expanded view; regular calendar and saved image/PDF materials stay unchanged. Keep opaque surfaces without backdrop support; reduced transparency, increased contrast and forced colors remove blur and shadow, with visible outlines for current events and selected tabs. No new visual identity or animation.

STORY: Choose School or Kindergarten per person and a timetable type. Calendar sources lists categories first, each with a compact picker containing calendar checkboxes. Each category accepts several calendars, and a calendar can belong to several categories. Day popup's selections for Activities, Meetings, Opening hours, Closure days and Messages (Pedagogy) also supply the selected day's activities on School; a settings hint states this shared use. Full-week sources remain independent. The inline agenda and day modals share the same categories and rendering: opening hours and closure status in the date header, Activities on the left, Meetings on the right and Messages (Pedagogy) across the bottom. Their closure calendars suppress their opening hours independently of the week. Only configured sections appear, and messages belong to the displayed date. Legacy popup selections migrate by their existing source role, with unspecified calendars placed under Activities. The education choice changes saved and expanded week headings and timetable descriptions, defaulting to School. Saved image pages survive switching types.

FIRST VIEWPORT: School shows one full-width card per person for every timetable type. Calendar cards lead with “Activities for {name}”, which remains accurate for any selected date. Reuse the installed Radix Tabs above one inline agenda, defaulting to the actual current date in the home's time zone and resetting to today when that date changes. Weekends add a separate current-day tab alongside the upcoming five weekdays. Reveal the initial today tab by scrolling only the horizontal tab list, without moving page focus. Keep the visible Today marker and semantic current date independent of tab selection, with visible keyboard focus and the shared tab keyboard behavior.

Each tab shows its weekday and date, configured opening hours or closed status, and a primary-colored meeting badge only for a positive count. Omit zero counters. Loading or unavailable meeting data shows status text instead of a count. Tabs switch the selected date and agenda inline. The selected-day header shows its full date and opening-hours/closure status; the live clock appears only when that date is today. Show full activity notes and messages for the selected date, with no future-message fallback. School calendar cards have no Expand timetable action; the saved-image/PDF viewer remains available.

The selected-day agenda and day modals share one layout. At a content width of 48rem or more, configured Activities and Meetings share a 3:1 row; either section alone uses the full width. Sections stack at smaller widths. At a content width of 30rem or more, activity rows place time in a leading column beside the title and full details; narrower rows place time above the title. Meetings retain time above the title. Messages (Pedagogy) spans the full bottom row, with paragraphs capped at 75ch; all-day messages omit the redundant time label. Opening-hours and closure rows retain notes behind Details; omit the opening-hours event title while preserving closure reasons. Timed events in progress pair primary emphasis with visible “Happening now” text and semantic current-time state; all-day events receive no active-time indication. Clocks use the existing 15-second family-context tick. Day modals retain their 1400px maximum width, full-width date header and one scrolling body, and show the clock only for today. Keep sections as headings and dividers inside the shared shell, with full activities, times, locations and notes; omit calendar-source labels from agenda items. Keep status Details usable, keyboard focus visible and restored on modal dismissal.

Dashboard previews and the expanded calendar retain the full weekly view, its independent sources and the existing day dialogs. Show five weekday columns on wide calendar panels; at a calendar-container width of 48rem or less, stack dated sections with each day heading above its events. Place one live clock beside the week label when the displayed week includes today. Opening hours and closure status sit under each day heading. Weekly rows share the agenda's current-event treatment and place time beside the title at a day-container width of 18rem or more, or above it at narrower widths. Weekly activity notes expand through the title disclosure, without a repeated Details line. Full-width messages beneath the week keep their existing next-message fallback.

On School, saved images and converted PDF pages retain their aspect ratio and fill the card width without a height cap. Inactive carousel pages occupy zero height so mixed portrait and landscape pages leave no blank space beneath the active page. Dashboard thumbnail sizing and the expanded saved-page viewer retain their existing behavior. School only presents; all administration, including uploads, lives in Settings → Timetables.

FORM: Local extension, code-led. Monday–Friday applies to the full-week views and regular weekday tabs; School adds the actual current day on weekends. Calendar fetching includes that current day alongside the upcoming school week. Only below-week messages on dashboard previews and the expanded calendar use today's entry or the next available event within a year, with the source date shown; School's selected-day agenda and day modals use their displayed date without that fallback. Preserve loading, unavailable and empty states separately, including tab summaries.

FINISH: Verify synthetic data on desktop, phone and portrait in both themes; finish with independent review and documentation against the incumbent system. No private calendar contents in fixtures or screenshots.
