# School timetables

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing School board and dashboard previews.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each person can use saved timetable pages or today's calendar agenda on School, with the full calendar week available on the dashboard and in the expanded view.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Afterglow's system typography, semantic palette and existing form controls. Each calendar card on School uses one outer glass shell around its heading, today's agenda and compact date strip. The shell, full-week timetable frames and day popups share the stronger illuminated glass rim, blur and outer depth when backdrop filtering is available. Muted day surfaces and current-date emphasis support the current activity's highlight. Apply this local exception on School, dashboard previews and the expanded view; regular calendar and saved image/PDF materials stay unchanged. Keep opaque surfaces without backdrop support; reduced transparency, increased contrast and forced colors remove blur and shadow, with visible outlines for current events and dates. No new visual identity or animation.

STORY: Choose School or Kindergarten per person and a timetable type. Calendar sources lists categories first, each with a compact picker containing calendar checkboxes. Each category accepts several calendars, and a calendar can belong to several categories. Day popup's selections for Activities, Meetings, Opening hours, Closure days and Messages (Pedagogy) also supply today's activities on School; a settings hint states this shared use. Full-week sources remain independent. The main agenda and day modals share the same categories and rendering: opening hours and closure status in the date header, Activities on the left, Meetings on the right and Messages (Pedagogy) across the bottom. Their closure calendars suppress their opening hours independently of the week. Only configured sections appear, and messages belong to the displayed date. Legacy popup selections migrate by their existing source role, with unspecified calendars placed under Activities. The education choice changes saved and expanded week headings and timetable descriptions, defaulting to School. Saved image pages survive switching types.

FIRST VIEWPORT: School shows one full-width card per person for every timetable type. Calendar cards lead with “Today's activities for {name}” and the actual current date in the home's time zone, including weekends. Show the live clock and opening-hours/closure status in the date header, followed by today's configured agenda with full notes. Main School messages are for today only, with no future-message fallback. Beneath the agenda, a compact strip shows five weekday dates without event previews; the current date is highlighted when present, and each date opens its full day modal. The strip scrolls horizontally on narrow screens and advances to the next school week on weekends while the main agenda continues to show the actual current day. Keep Expand timetable available for the full week.

Today's agenda and day modals share one layout. At a content width of 48rem or more, configured Activities and Meetings share a 3:1 row; either section alone uses the full width. Sections stack at smaller widths. At a content width of 30rem or more, activity rows place time in a leading column beside the title and full details; narrower rows place time above the title. Meetings retain time above the title. Messages (Pedagogy) spans the full bottom row, with paragraphs capped at 75ch; all-day messages omit the redundant time label. Opening-hours and closure rows retain notes behind Details; omit the opening-hours event title while preserving closure reasons. Timed events in progress pair primary emphasis with visible “Happening now” text and semantic current-time state; all-day events receive no active-time indication. Clocks use the existing 15-second family-context tick. Day modals retain their 1400px maximum width, full-width date header and one scrolling body, and show the clock only for today. Keep sections as headings and dividers inside the shared shell, with full activities, times, locations and notes; omit calendar-source labels from agenda items. Keep status Details usable, keyboard focus visible and restored on dismissal, and day modals available inside the expanded timetable.

Dashboard previews and the expanded calendar retain the full weekly view and its independent sources. Show five weekday columns on wide calendar panels; at a calendar-container width of 48rem or less, stack dated sections with each day heading above its events. Place one live clock beside the week label when the displayed week includes today. Opening hours and closure status sit under each day heading. Weekly rows share the agenda's current-event treatment and place time beside the title at a day-container width of 18rem or more, or above it at narrower widths. Weekly activity notes expand through the title disclosure, without a repeated Details line. Full-width messages beneath the week keep their existing next-message fallback.

On School, saved images and converted PDF pages retain their aspect ratio and fill the card width without a height cap. Inactive carousel pages occupy zero height so mixed portrait and landscape pages leave no blank space beneath the active page. Dashboard thumbnail sizing and the expanded saved-page viewer retain their existing behavior. School only presents; all administration, including uploads, lives in Settings → Timetables.

FORM: Local extension, code-led. Monday–Friday applies to the date strip and full-week views; the main School agenda includes weekends. Calendar fetching includes the actual current day even when the weekday strip has advanced to next Monday. Only below-week messages on dashboard previews and the expanded calendar use today's entry or the next available event within a year, with the source date shown; today's School agenda and day modals use their displayed date without that fallback. Preserve loading, unavailable and empty states separately.

FINISH: Verify synthetic data on desktop, phone and portrait in both themes; finish with independent review and documentation against the incumbent system. No private calendar contents in fixtures or screenshots.
