# Shared household board

Mode: Operate. Primary target: 1440 × 2560 portrait touchscreen; phone and desktop layouts remain supported. The experiment changes the app's shared visual system while preserving workflows, content, safe commands, and data ownership.

Favorites extension: Today reuses super compact `SmartHomeCard` surfaces as separate cards with gaps. Favorite cards and scene actions use 20px corners, including picker previews and unavailable placeholders; their outer panels remain 24px. The customization dialog separates its vertically scrollable three-column card catalogue and filtered entity picker in a shared glass inset. Selected favorites appear beneath it as actual inert card previews with icons and reported states, with searchable group dropdowns and removal actions directly underneath. A dedicated Card groups section creates reusable names and removes groups; removing a group leaves its cards ungrouped, and empty groups persist. Their grid adapts from three columns to two to one; missing favorites retain a named unavailable surface. Matching names collect cards under headings on Today; the Ungrouped choice leaves cards ungrouped, with no group heading when all cards are ungrouped. The catalogue previews shrink on phones while the catalogue grid remains three across. Type labels use the full column width above a compact availability count; accessible labels retain the type and available count. Previews omit secondary facts; selection survives type changes, missing entities and renames through stable preference IDs. Save and Cancel remain outside the scrolling body. Super compact scenes use a low row with a name, icon and activation button. Today’s favorites and Scenes center incomplete rows at the same item width as a full row, including mixed favorite groups. Items keep their source order. This is a user-specified extension of the existing system, with no new visual world or assets.

The optional Homework panel groups linked lists by person and names the due period in its heading. Through today and Through tomorrow include earlier deadlines. Homework without a due date is included by default in every period and labeled when viewing a particular period. In check-off mode, reuse the existing list controls. In either link mode, use static rows with one destination in the heading. Completed work carries a checkmark and an accessible completion label when shown.

The optional Recent at home section (off by default) reuses the Smart home activity panel unchanged: opaque work surface, divided rows, the same loading, unavailable, quiet-day, no-source and stale states. It is full width by default and may be half; it follows the household’s section order; sources and the people choice stay in Settings → System → Activity.

Panel layout extension: Customize Today gives every section except Everyone’s day, Everyone’s week and School a Full | Half width; consecutive halves pair into rows (the Paired Rows Rule in DESIGN.md), partners stretch to one height and an empty partner lets the other span its row. An inert preview of the rows sits at the top of the dialog. One list-like section (Recent at home, Daily rhythm, Shopping or Homework; none by default) may fill the large wall’s free space: its row is sized from the rest of the board and the dock, and the panel adds whole rows that fit, never hiding the rows it normally shows. The special landscape week layout is retired; landscape uses the same rows.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An iOS Liquid Glass inspired control layer floats above a readable household board.

OWN-WORLD: Cool pearl and midnight blue grounds, native system typography, crisp blue actions, opaque rounded work surfaces. Translucent navigation has a softly illuminated rim and a restrained shadow; content never competes with the controls.

STORY: Read the next plan, scan the family week, act on lists, inspect school and home controls. Keep the five labeled destinations and visible offline, pending, and error states.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The compact brand header leads into Today, its settings, the date and the clock. Honor the household’s chosen section order. The next plan names the activity and person, emphasizes the time, and links to that day in Calendar. Weather defaults beside Today’s clock: an open, larger icon-and-temperature composition with the reported condition; tapping it opens the forecast. Sun and partial cloud use the existing warm semantic accent; other conditions use blue. Settings → Weather selects the weather source and an optional separate temperature entity, and independently enables Beside the clock and In the header, including both or neither. Beside the clock follows the clock: beside Today’s large clock, and as the compact header widget beside the header clock that replaces the wordmark on other pages (phones keep only the time there). The optional entity supplies only the current temperature with its reported unit; forecast values retain the weather source’s units. A missing selected temperature reading stays explicit instead of silently switching sources. The optional header uses a compact glass control. Both open the existing supported forecast popover, and missing/offline data stays explicit. Phones put Today and the date on one line when they fit, with the clock and weather together below. An enabled header widget retains its separate compact arrangement. All choices sync across screens. Lists, forms and calendar rows remain opaque.

FORM: User pinned iOS Liquid Glass and asked to implement an experiment on a branch. This overrides the direction roll (seed:04333850e6d199d9); code-led, no decision comp. Sources: Apple's Materials HIG and WWDC25 Meet Liquid Glass. CSS supplies blur, translucency and specular edges; native optical refraction is outside the web implementation.

FINISH: Verify light/dark, desktop, phone, portrait, navigation, lists and dialogs with fictional fixtures; preserve reduced motion and provide solid surfaces for reduced transparency, increased contrast and missing backdrop-filter support. Finish with independent review and matching design documentation.
